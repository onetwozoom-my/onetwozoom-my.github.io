About Onetwozoom

Located in Setapak, Kuala Lumpur, Onetwozoom is a premier professional photo studio with over 30 years of trusted experience. We specialize in high-quality photo printing, urgent passport and visa photos, and personalized studio portraits, combining decades of technical expertise with a warm, neighborhood touch.

Beyond our KL walk-in store, we offer top-tier custom framing, bespoke photo albums, and vibrant sticker printing to customers nationwide via Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop. Backed by countless 5-star reviews for our secure packaging and true-to-life print quality, Onetwozoom seamlessly blends traditional photography excellence with modern e-commerce convenience to help you capture and keep your best memories for a lifetime.
